<b>ObminFX</b> is a free, open-source currency converter and exchange rate app that also shows what your bank card really charges you abroad. "Obmin" means "exchange" in Ukrainian.

Your bank shows one exchange rate, then bills you another plus a foreign transaction fee. ObminFX puts the official rate, the market rate and your card's real cost side by side, so you know the price before you pay.

<b>Who it's for</b>
<ul>
<li>Travelers paying by card in another currency</li>
<li>Online shoppers buying from foreign stores</li>
<li>Digital nomads, freelancers and expats paid in one currency and spending in another</li>
<li>Anyone in Ukraine who wants the official NBU exchange rate (курс НБУ) at a glance</li>
</ul>

<b>Live exchange rates from sources you can name</b>
<ul>
<li>European Central Bank (ECB) daily reference rates</li>
<li>National Bank of Ukraine (NBU) official hryvnia rates</li>
<li>Open-market mid-rates for 160+ world currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, PLN, UAH, CAD, JPY, CHF and more</li>
<li>Crypto prices: Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), Tether (USDT)</li>
<li>Gold, silver, platinum and palladium prices</li>
<li>Mastercard and Visa rate estimates (mid-rate plus a typical network spread)</li>
</ul>

<b>Card fee calculator</b>
<ul>
<li>Simulate a purchase with your card's foreign transaction fee (0% to 3.5%+) and see the total you'll be billed</li>
<li>Check my card: enter the amounts from your bank app to find the real markup you paid compared with the NBU or ECB rate</li>
<li>Share a bank SMS or notification to ObminFX and it fills in the numbers for you</li>
<li>Save your cards and see which one is cheapest for each purchase</li>
</ul>

<b>Fast, simple converter</b>
<ul>
<li>Type an amount once and see it in all your currencies</li>
<li>Pin favorite currencies to the top; switch the base currency with one tap</li>
<li>Exchange rate history charts (ECB and NBU)</li>
<li>Works offline with your last saved rates</li>
<li>Light and dark theme; English and Ukrainian</li>
<li>Short tour on first launch, which you can skip</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
No ads, no account, no analytics or tracking. Your currencies, cards and settings stay on your phone. The app only goes online to download exchange rates, from public sources: Frankfurter/ECB, bank.gov.ua, open.er-api.com, CoinGecko and gold-api.com.
