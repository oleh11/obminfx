# Privacy Policy for ObminFX

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026

**ObminFX** ("the App") is developed as a free, privacy-first currency conversion and bank fee simulation application. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the App.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Personal Information
ObminFX **does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data**. 
- No user accounts or registrations are required.
- We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
- We do not access contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID).

### Location Data
The App **does not track or request your GPS or device location**.

### Financial and Payment Information
ObminFX contains a bank fee calculator and currency converter. All calculations and simulations are performed entirely on your local device. The App **never accesses or collects credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial credentials**.

---

## 2. Network Requests & Third-Party APIs

To provide live exchange rates, ObminFX queries public exchange rate APIs:
- European Central Bank (ECB)
- National Bank of Ukraine (NBU)
- Global Open Exchange Rate API (`open.er-api.com`)

These network requests transmit only standard anonymous HTTP headers (such as user-agent) necessary to fetch rate tables. No personal identifiers or search queries are transmitted.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage

All user preferences (such as selected base currency, favorite pinned currencies, and default bank fee percentage) are stored **exclusively on your local device** using Android Local Storage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings.

---

## 4. Advertising & Analytics

- **No Ads:** ObminFX does not display third-party advertisements.
- **No Third-Party Trackers:** We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any behavioral tracking SDKs.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

ObminFX does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
